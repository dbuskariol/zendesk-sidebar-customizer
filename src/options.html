Zendesk Views Tweaks

Compact the views sidebar and hide views or groups you don't care about.

Settings

Density (Compact mode)

Per-level font size and indent. Applies only when Compact mode is on. Indent controls the left padding added to nested children at that level.

Views & groups

Uncheck a leaf view to hide just that view. Uncheck a group to hide the entire group (header and all children). Changes apply live.

Add a view manually

Paste a view URL (e.g. https://github.zendesk.com/agent/filters/360001234567) or just the numeric ID. Useful for hiding a view you haven't loaded yet.
